**Spooky Chest** is a game about a (obviously) a chest that needs to catch all the ghost that are escaped from him.

As you know, chest can't move by themself, so you need to rotate the entire room and use the gravity to shift him and made him falling on to every little ghost in all the levels!

## Commands

Left / Right: Rotate the room counterclockwise / clockwise
R: Restart level

If you use a device with the touchscreen, you can tap on the left and right side of the game to rotate the room counterclockwise and clockwise
